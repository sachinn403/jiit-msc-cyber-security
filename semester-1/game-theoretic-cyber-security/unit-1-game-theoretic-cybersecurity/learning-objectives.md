# Learning Objectives

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

* Understand the **fundamental concepts of Game Theory** and its role in strategic decision-making.
* Explain the **game-theoretic background of cybersecurity** and why cyber conflicts can be modeled as games.
* Identify the **key elements of a game**, including players, strategies, and payoffs.
* Recognize how **Game Theory** is applied to analyze attacker–defender interactions in cybersecurity.
* Differentiate between the **various game-theoretic models** used in cybersecurity and understand their applications.
* Develop a foundational understanding of how game-theoretic techniques support cyber defense and security decision-making.
