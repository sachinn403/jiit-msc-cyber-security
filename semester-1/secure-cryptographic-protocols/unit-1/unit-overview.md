# 📖 Unit Overview

### 📖 Unit 1 Overview

* Introduces the fundamentals of secure cryptographic protocols.
* Explains the goals of information security and cryptography.
* Covers basic information theory concepts such as entropy and mutual information.
* Describes different attacker models and threat scenarios.
* Introduces common cyber threats, including brute-force attacks and password attacks.
* Explains the difference between encryption, hashing, and encoding.
* Provides the theoretical foundation for advanced cryptographic algorithms and secure communication.
