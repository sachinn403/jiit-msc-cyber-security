**Course:** Foundations of Secure Cryptographic Protocols

**Course Code:** 26M11MA111

### Module 1 — Foundations of Security, Uncertainty and Threat Models

**Lectures:** 8

* Security objectives as uncertainty reduction and leakage control

* One-Time Pad

* Password entropy
* Threat landscape
